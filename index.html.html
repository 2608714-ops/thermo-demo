Knowledge check

Equations and symbols (SI)

pV = nRT
W_by = ∫ p dV
ΔU = Q − W_by
ΔU = n C_V ΔT
Isothermal: W = nRT ln(V₂/V₁)
Isobaric: W = p(V₂ − V₁)
Isochoric: W = 0

p pressure (Pa) · V volume (m³) · n amount (mol) · T absolute temperature (K) · R = 8.314 J/(mol·K) · Q heat added to gas (J) · Wby work done by gas (J) · ΔU change in internal energy (J) · CV molar heat capacity at constant volume (J/(mol·K)).

How to use

  • Choose a process, then drag the piston (or the thermometer when the volume is locked) or use the sliders.
  • Press Play to move the gas from state 1 to state 2. Watch the molecules, the flame or ice, and the energy bars.
  • Compare the totals with the worked calculation, then load the three test cases in the verification table.
  • Sign convention: Wby > 0 when the gas expands. Q > 0 when heat enters the gas.

Assumptions and limits

  • The gas is ideal: low density and far from condensation. The demo warns below 150 K.
  • Each process is slow and reversible, so p and T are uniform at every step.
  • The amount of gas n is fixed. No leaks.
  • CV is constant (3R/2 or 5R/2). Real gases change CV at very high or low temperature.
  • Friction and heat loss through the walls are ignored.
  • The molecule animation is a picture of the idea. It shows speed and density but is not a real molecular simulation, and the flame and ice only show the direction and rough size of Q.
  • Temperatures are converted to kelvin before every calculation.
